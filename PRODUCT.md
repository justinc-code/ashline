# Ashline
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React + Vite + TypeScript, confirmed by user.
## Users
Emergency teams and local residents, using separate Operations and Community views.
## Product Purpose
Training prototype for volcanic unrest exploration, hazard simulation, and loss mitigation. Eruptions cannot currently be prevented.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Worldwide Holocene catalog; synthetic signals; shared model with type presets; approximate hazard zones and staged action plans; browser saves and JSON/print export. No operational forecasts or alerts.
## Evidence on Hand
Official Smithsonian GVP Holocene WFS snapshot retrieved 2026-09-07: all 1,214 returned records, validated against totalFeatures, bundled in src/data/volcanoes.json. Natural Earth 110m land geometry, public domain. Manrope font self-hosted under SIL OFL.
## Product Principles
Distinguish observation from simulation. Make uncertainty visible. Prefer actionable preparedness. Preserve source provenance.
